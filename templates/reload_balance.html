Reload Your Balance

Last Updated: January 2025

Welcome to Sydney Market! This guide will walk you through the process of adding funds to your account balance using Bitcoin (BTC) or Monero (XMR). Your balance can be used to purchase products from our trusted vendors.

1. Why Use Account Balance?

  • Faster Checkout: No need to enter payment details for each purchase
  • Better Privacy: Single deposit transaction instead of multiple payments
  • Escrow Protection: All purchases are protected by our multisig escrow system
  • Instant Availability: Funds are available immediately after confirmation
  • Lower Fees: Avoid multiple transaction fees from frequent small payments

2. Supported Cryptocurrencies

Bitcoin (BTC)

  • • Minimum deposit: 0.001 BTC
  • • Network fee: ~$1-5 USD
  • • Confirmation time: 1-3 blocks (~10-30 minutes)
  • • Widely accepted and trusted

Monero (XMR)

  • • Minimum deposit: 0.01 XMR
  • • Network fee: ~$0.01-0.05 USD
  • • Confirmation time: 10 blocks (~20 minutes)
  • • Enhanced privacy and fungibility

3. How to Reload Your Balance

Step-by-Step Process:

  1. Login to Your Account: Ensure you're logged into your Sydney Market account
  2. Navigate to Balance: Go to your dashboard and click on "Balance" in the Profile Actions sidebar
  3. Choose Cryptocurrency: Select either Bitcoin (BTC) or Monero (XMR) for your deposit
  4. Generate Deposit Address: Click "Generate New Address" to get a unique deposit address
  5. Send Payment: Use your wallet to send the desired amount to the provided address
  6. Wait for Confirmation: Monitor the transaction status - funds will be credited after network confirmations
  7. Start Shopping: Once confirmed, your balance will be updated and ready for purchases

4. Security Best Practices

✅ Do's:

  • Always verify the deposit address before sending
  • Use a secure, private wallet for transactions
  • Double-check the amount before confirming
  • Keep your wallet software updated
  • Use PGP encryption for sensitive communications
  • Enable 2FA on your Sydney Market account

❌ Don'ts:

  • Never share your private keys or seed phrases
  • Don't send funds from exchanges (use personal wallets)
  • Avoid using public WiFi for transactions
  • Don't reuse deposit addresses
  • Never send funds to addresses from unverified sources
  • Don't store large amounts in hot wallets

5. Understanding Transaction Status

Pending: Transaction sent but not yet confirmed by the network
Confirming: Transaction has been included in a block, waiting for additional confirmations
Confirmed: Transaction fully confirmed, funds added to your balance
Failed: Transaction failed or was rejected by the network

6. Troubleshooting Common Issues

Transaction Not Appearing?

Check that you sent to the correct address and that the transaction has sufficient confirmations. Bitcoin requires 1-3 confirmations, Monero requires 10 confirmations.

Wrong Amount Received?

Network fees are deducted from your sent amount. The amount credited will be slightly less than what you sent due to transaction fees.

Long Confirmation Times?

Network congestion can cause delays. Higher fees can speed up confirmation times. Be patient and don't resend the transaction.

Still Having Issues?

Contact our support team with your transaction ID (TXID) and we'll help you resolve the issue.

7. Important Notes

  • No Refunds: Deposits are non-refundable. Only send amounts you intend to spend.
  • Address Expiry: Deposit addresses expire after 24 hours for security. Generate a new address if needed.
  • Minimum Amounts: Deposits below the minimum will not be credited to your account.
  • Network Fees: You are responsible for paying network transaction fees.
  • Price Volatility: Cryptocurrency prices fluctuate. Your balance value may change between deposit and use.
  • Security: Sydney Market uses industry-standard security measures, but you should always use secure practices.

8. Need Help?

If you need assistance with reloading your balance or have questions about the process:

  • Check our FAQ section for common questions
  • Contact support through the messaging system
  • Send a PGP-encrypted message to our support team
  • Include your username and transaction details when seeking help
