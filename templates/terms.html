Privacy Policy

Your Privacy is Our Priority

Tor Network PGP Encrypted No IP Logging
Last Updated: February 2025

Introduction

Sydney Marketplace operates on the Tor network, prioritizing user privacy, anonymity, and security above all else. This Privacy Policy outlines our commitment to protecting your digital rights and explains how we handle information in our privacy-focused marketplace environment.

🔒 Core Privacy Principles:

  • • No IP address collection or logging
  • • PGP encryption for all communications
  • • Cryptocurrency-only transactions (BTC/XMR)
  • • Minimal data retention policies
  • • Zero third-party data sharing

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

  • Username: Your chosen marketplace identifier
  • Password Hash: Securely hashed using bcrypt
  • PGP Public Key: For encrypted communications
  • Cryptocurrency Addresses: BTC/XMR addresses for transactions
  • Vendor Bond: Escrow bond information (vendors only)

1.2 Transaction Data

  • Order Details: Product information, quantities, prices
  • Escrow Addresses: Multisig wallet addresses
  • Transaction Status: Order progress tracking
  • Dispute Records: Resolution documentation

1.3 What We DON'T Collect

  • ❌ Real names or personal identifiers
  • ❌ Email addresses or phone numbers
  • ❌ IP addresses or location data
  • ❌ Browser fingerprints or device IDs
  • ❌ Social media accounts or external profiles

2. How We Use Your Information

✅ Legitimate Uses

  • • Process marketplace transactions
  • • Facilitate buyer-vendor communication
  • • Manage escrow and dispute resolution
  • • Prevent fraud and platform abuse
  • • Maintain marketplace security

❌ What We Never Do

  • • Sell or rent your data
  • • Share with third parties
  • • Use for marketing purposes
  • • Track across other sites
  • • Create user profiles

3. Security & Privacy Measures

Encryption & Security

  • PGP Encryption: All messages end-to-end encrypted
  • Password Hashing: bcrypt with high cost factor
  • Session Security: Secure session management
  • Database Encryption: AES-256 for sensitive data
  • Tor Network: Anonymous routing and access

Data Retention

  • Active Accounts: Data retained while active
  • Inactive Accounts: Deleted after 12 months
  • Transaction Logs: Deleted after 12 hours
  • Messages: Auto-delete after 30 days
  • Backup Rotation: 7-day retention maximum

4. Cryptocurrency Privacy

Bitcoin (BTC) Considerations

Bitcoin transactions are recorded on a public blockchain. While we anonymize your identity on our platform, blockchain data (addresses, amounts, timestamps) is permanent and publicly accessible.

Privacy Best Practices:

  • • Use new addresses for each transaction
  • • Consider using Bitcoin mixers or CoinJoin
  • • Avoid linking multiple transactions to same address
  • • Use hardware wallets for enhanced security

Monero (XMR) Privacy

Monero provides enhanced privacy through ring signatures, stealth addresses, and RingCT. Transaction amounts and addresses are obfuscated by default.

Monero Advantages:

  • • Ring signatures hide sender identity
  • • Stealth addresses protect recipient privacy
  • • RingCT obscures transaction amounts
  • • Built-in privacy by default
