Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about advertising on {{ settings['site_name'] }}

How much should I bid per click?

Bids typically range from 0.0001 to 0.001 BTC per click, depending on your product category and competition. Start with a moderate bid (0.0005 BTC) and adjust based on performance. Higher bids increase your ad's visibility and placement position.

What's the minimum budget I need?

The minimum budget is 0.001 BTC, but we recommend starting with at least 0.01 BTC for meaningful results. This gives you enough clicks to gather data and optimize your campaign effectively.

How do I know if my campaign is working?

Track your campaign's click-through rate (CTR), conversion rate, and cost per acquisition (CPA). A good CTR is 2-5%, and you should aim for a CPA that's 20-30% of your product price for profitability.

Can I pause or stop my campaign anytime?

Yes! You have full control over your campaigns. You can pause, resume, or stop them at any time. Unused budget remains in your advertising wallet and can be used for future campaigns or withdrawn.

What happens if I run out of budget?

When your daily budget is reached, your ad stops showing for that day. It automatically resumes the next day if you have sufficient funds. You can also increase your budget mid-campaign to continue advertising.

How do I get the best ROI from my ads?

Focus on high-quality product images, compelling descriptions, competitive pricing, and target relevant categories. Monitor your analytics regularly and adjust bids based on performance. Consider A/B testing different ad creatives.
