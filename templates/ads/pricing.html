Frequently Asked Questions

How does the bidding system work?

Ads are displayed based on bid amount and placement priority. Higher bids get better visibility. You only pay for actual impressions, and unused budget is refunded.

Can I pause my campaign?

Yes, you can pause, resume, or end your campaign at any time. Paused campaigns don't incur charges.

What happens to unused budget?

Any unused budget is automatically refunded to your wallet when the campaign ends or is cancelled.

How do I track performance?

Real-time analytics show impressions, clicks, click-through rates, and costs. Data is available in your ads dashboard.
