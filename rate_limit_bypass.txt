
# Rate Limit Bypass Instructions
# 
# The issue is that the DDoS protection system is blocking your requests.
# Here are several ways to fix this:

# Method 1: Restart Flask with rate limiting disabled
# Add this to your .env file:
DDOS_ENABLED=false
RATE_LIMIT_ENABLED=false

# Method 2: Increase rate limits in .env file:
DDOS_MAX_REQUESTS_PER_MINUTE=10000
DDOS_MAX_REQUESTS_PER_HOUR=50000
DDOS_MAX_REQUESTS_PER_DAY=100000
DDOS_BURST_LIMIT=1000

# Method 3: Add your IP to whitelist in .env file:
DDOS_WHITELIST_IPS=127.0.0.1,::1,YOUR_IP_ADDRESS

# Method 4: Clear Redis manually:
# redis-cli FLUSHALL

# After making changes, restart your Flask application.
